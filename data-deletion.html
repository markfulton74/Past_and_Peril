Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Past and Peril respects your right to request deletion of personal information associated with your use of the application.

How to Request Data Deletion

To request deletion of your personal information from Past and Peril, send an email to:

mwfulton@gmail.com

Please use the subject line:

Past and Peril Data Deletion Request

To help us identify the appropriate account, please include the email address associated with your Past and Peril account or the relevant Facebook or Threads account identifier.

Do not send your password or other authentication credentials.

What Happens After You Submit a Request

We will review the request and, where applicable, delete personal information that we are not required or permitted to retain.

Some information may need to be retained where required by law, for legitimate security purposes, to prevent fraud or abuse, or to resolve disputes.

Facebook and Threads

If you connected Past and Peril to Facebook or Threads, you may also remove the application's permissions through the relevant Meta account settings.

Removing permissions through Facebook or Threads may prevent future access to information, but it does not necessarily delete information that Past and Peril may already have stored.

For deletion from Past and Peril's systems, please use the email request process described above.

Contact

If you have questions about data deletion, please contact:

mwfulton@gmail.com
